# ADR-003: Use Auth0 as the identity provider for Meridian Phase 1

## Context
Meridian Phase 1 includes unified identity across web and mobile while the platform is still coexisting with 22 regional stacks and multiple CRMs. The program already carries enough transformation risk that identity should not become a custom platform the junior internal team must design and harden from scratch.

## Decision
We will use Auth0 as the identity provider for Meridian Phase 1 and keep identity orchestration inside Meridian limited to integration, token/session handling, and role/context mapping.

## Alternatives Considered
1. **Build an in-house identity platform** — rejected because Meridian would be taking on security, compliance, and operational complexity that does not help Phase 1 move faster or safer.
2. **Leave identity fragmented by region in Phase 1** — rejected because it weakens the goal of unified web/mobile/cart/checkout experience and increases migration complexity across channels and markets.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** Accelerates unified identity delivery, reduces custom security/platform burden, and gives Phase 1 a clearer path to cross-channel session consistency.
- **Negative:** Creates vendor dependency and limits how much Meridian can customize identity internals compared with a fully in-house stack.

## Agent-Readable Summary
Auth0 is the external identity provider for authentication and token flows in Meridian Phase 1. Do not build or introduce a parallel in-house identity authority for login, token issuance, or session truth; custom Meridian services may orchestrate identity context but must not replace Auth0 as the identity source.
