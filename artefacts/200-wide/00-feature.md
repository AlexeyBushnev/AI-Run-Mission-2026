Product: IoT Data Quality Platform for legacy-to-PySpark migration and reporting validation.
Feature: Parity Investigation Assistant.
Target user: data quality engineers and data engineers investigating parity defects in migrated IoT pipelines.
Expected behaviour: when a parity defect is detected, the engineer runs one AI-assisted investigation flow that produces a reviewed root-cause hypothesis and reusable evidence summary.
Success signal: average parity-investigation time decreases while evidence-review acceptance stays stable.
Out of scope: automatic production fixes, automatic defect closure, or final engineering decision without human review.
