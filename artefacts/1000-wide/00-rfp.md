# Meridian Retail Group — Omnichannel Checkout Modernization and AI-Assisted Cart Summary RFP

## Buyer & decision
The buyer is **Meridian Retail Group**. The decision they are making is **which delivery partner will design, build, secure, operate, and support a modernized checkout service and AI-assisted cart-summary capability for Meridian’s retail channels**.

## Objective
Meridian seeks a delivery partner to improve checkout reliability, reduce cart abandonment, support omnichannel retail growth, and introduce a governed AI-assisted “summarise my cart” feature without increasing operational or security risk beyond approved limits. Success means a production-ready checkout platform with measurable improvement over the current no-AI baseline in conversion, latency, supportability, and deployment discipline, together with a clear operating model, cost control, and evidence pack suitable for governance review.

## Scope

### In scope
- Modernization of the checkout-service flow for web and mobile channels
- API, service, and integration design for cart and checkout operations
- AI-assisted cart-summary feature through Meridian’s approved model gateway
- Deployment architecture, CI/CD workflow, and rollback approach
- Security threat model, mitigation plan, and evidence pack
- Cloud operations and support pack, including runbook, cost cap, and readiness brief
- Hypercare and transition to Meridian support ownership

### Out of scope
- Re-platforming the full e-commerce estate outside checkout and cart flows
- ERP, POS, or warehouse modernization beyond required integration touchpoints
- Enterprise-wide data-lake or analytics transformation
- Non-approved model vendors or unmanaged direct model access
- Business-policy decisions on risk acceptance, AI governance classification, or legal sign-off

## Constraints
- **Timeline:** production-ready release required within the buyer’s target program window; bidder must provide a phased plan with milestones and rollback readiness
- **Commercial model:** bidder must state recommended commercial model and assumptions clearly
- **Regulatory / governance:** solution must support Meridian governance, security review, auditability, and controlled use of AI through an approved gateway
- **Technology:** containerized service deployment, CI/CD controls, observability, database and cache integration, and approved AI gateway usage are required
- **Sourcing:** bidder may propose subcontracted capability only if governance, accountability, and evidence ownership are explicit

## Evaluation criteria (with weights)
| Criterion | Weight |
